🤓 Guide: Add Your Own Handwriting Font

  1. Visit a free font generator site like Calligraphr.
  2. Download & print their handwriting template grid sheet.
  3. Fill in your letters using a black gel pen and scan/photograph it.
  4. Upload your completed template to generate your personalized .ttf font file.
  5. Click "Upload Custom Font" in the options above!
